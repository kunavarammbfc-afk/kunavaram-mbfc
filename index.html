About Mahesh Babu

The Prince of Telugu Cinema

Personal Life

Mahesh Babu, born on August 9, 1975, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, is the son of legendary Telugu actor Krishna and Vijaya Nirmala. He grew up in the film industry and started his career as a child artist before making his debut as a lead actor in "Rajakumarudu" in 1999.

Career Highlights

With over 25 years in the industry, Mahesh Babu has delivered numerous blockbusters including Murari, Okkadu, Pokiri, Dookudu, Businessman, Srimanthudu, Bharat Ane Nenu, Maharshi, Sarileru Neekevvaru, and Sarkaru Vaari Paata. He has won 9 Nandi Awards and 5 Filmfare Awards South.

Philanthropy

Beyond cinema, Mahesh Babu is known for his charitable work. He has adopted the village of Buripalem in Andhra Pradesh and Siddhapuram in Telangana, and has been actively involved in various social causes including education and healthcare initiatives.

First Mother's Milk Bank

A major milestone in child healthcare was achieved as Namrata Shirodkar, trustee of the Mahesh Babu Foundation, inaugurated Andhra Pradesh's first Mothers' Milk Bank at Andhra Hospitals, Vijayawada. In collaboration with Rotary Club of Vijayawada and Rotary International, the milk bank provides safe, pasteurized donor breast milk to newborns whose mothers are unable to breastfeed, helping over 7,200 infants annually.

Children Cardiac Surgical Camp

The Mahesh Babu Foundation organized a Children Cardiac Surgical Camp in collaboration with Healing Little Hearts, UK and Andhra Hospitals. Four children underwent successful heart surgery during the camp on February 20, 2024. All children recovered well and were discharged, giving them a second chance at life.

Menstrual Hygiene Awareness

The Mahesh Babu Foundation conducted awareness programs on menstrual hygiene for orphan kids. This initiative aims to educate young girls about personal hygiene and health, breaking the stigma around menstruation and ensuring a healthier future for underprivileged children.

Saving Hearts

The Mahesh Babu Foundation has funded over 7000+ heart surgeries for children from underprivileged families in collaboration with Andhra Hospitals and Rainbow Hospitals. Inspired by his own son Gautam's premature birth experience, Mahesh Babu has been providing financial assistance to families with newborn babies diagnosed with heart ailments.

Free Medical Camps

The Foundation conducts almost 100+ free medical camps every month across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. These multispeciality health camps provide free consultations, diagnostic investigations, and essential medicines to rural communities. The 45th mega multispeciality camp at Burripalem alone benefited 132 adults and children.

Scholarships

The Mahesh Babu Foundation provides 50+ scholarships for medical students through the Superstar Krishna Scholarship Fund. This initiative supports meritorious and underprivileged students across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana in achieving their academic dreams and gaining crucial skills for their future.

Bringing Sound to Young Lives

Mahesh Babu Foundation has received FCRA approval and collaborated with NATS Foundation for the first time to support underprivileged children with hearing disabilities. This groundbreaking initiative helps them hear a brighter tomorrow, ensuring every child has access to the gift of sound and a brighter future for the next generation.

Child Health Initiatives

The Foundation regularly organizes mega child health camps in adopted villages. A recent camp at Burripalem with Andhra Hospitals benefited 115 children with free checkups and medicines. MB Foundation also conducted a Children Cardiac Surgical Camp with Healing Little Hearts, UK, where four children underwent successful heart surgery.

HPV Vaccination Drives

The Mahesh Babu Foundation conducted free HPV vaccination drives in Vijayawada for girls, promoting preventive healthcare and women's empowerment. This initiative aims to protect young girls from cervical cancer and ensure a healthier future for the next generation.

Our Story

The Kunavaram Mahesh Babu Fans Club (KMBFC) was established with a vision to bring together fans of Superstar Mahesh Babu in and around Kunavaram. Our club is not just about watching movies — it's about building a community that shares the same passion and admiration for the Prince of Telugu Cinema.

Since our inception, we have organized numerous events, celebrations, and charitable activities that reflect the values our idol stands for. From grand movie celebrations to community service, we strive to make a positive impact in our society.
